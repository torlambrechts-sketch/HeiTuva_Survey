## 4. Scope NOT delivered, with reasons

**Q58 — RETURNED, not built.** Confirmed as a pack property, but checking the seeded rows
rather than the plan found three things that make the confirmed form unbuildable as written:
only **two** packs carry a locked policy at all (`psykososial-kartlegging` k=5,
`leverandor-apenhetsloven` k=0); **`trakassering-ytringsklima` carries none**, so giving it 8
also means giving it `locked`/`anonymity`/`respondent_kind` — bringing it under Q17's lock for
the first time; and **«varsling» and «helse» have no pack**. Further, the floor-not-ceiling
requirement needs a **code change** after all: `app.apply_pack_policy` assigns and returns
early, so a locked pack never consults the organisation default and a pack carrying 8 would
**lower** an org whose default is 10 — the outcome the requirement forbids. My Batch B claim
of "no code change at all" was right that the mechanism exists and wrong that it implements
the semantics. Full detail: `docs/v2/05-batches.md § Batch F`… § Q58.

**«Profil og avsender» — NOT in this phase.** The plan paired it with Q38 and that was wrong,
for a reason that is about the standing rules rather than about size: of the tab's four
cards, **sender domains with DNS verification state** is the surface v2 draws, and verifying a
domain is an SES/DNS integration — deferred to V2-11. The standing constraint is that no
earlier phase may depend on anything the deferred phase would deliver, and the plan's own
mitigation ("manual/DNS-record display only") produces a card that displays records nobody can
check, next to a status chip that would have to be fabricated. **CLAUDE.md's never-fabricate
rule decides it.** Logo slots need Storage and accent/type overrides need columns; neither is
blocked, but neither is worth a phase on its own. **Recommendation: fold the unblocked half
into V2-2 and leave sender verification with V2-11.**
