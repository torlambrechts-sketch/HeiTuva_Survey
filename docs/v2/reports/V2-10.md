## 2. THERE IS NO `quiz_attempts` TABLE — the phase's main decision

`03-plan.md` proposed `quiz_attempts(survey_id, member_id, attempt_no, score, passed, taken_at)`.
Q84 removed `passed` and `attempt_no`. **Q61's confirmed «derive, do not duplicate» removed the
rest:** a quiz runs on a NAMED survey (Q85 makes the database say so) and the answer key is on
the question, so a score is a *function of rows this database already holds*. Storing it would
be storing a derived fact beside its source — the thing Q61 refused for member status, and for
the same reason.

**And the privacy answer is stronger than the plan's own test.** The plan's **test 2** was
«assert the absence of the FK and of any query joining `quiz_attempts` to `responses`».

> **That test did not pass. It became UNNECESSARY** — which is the better outcome, because
> **a test asserting a join does not exist is weaker than a schema in which it cannot be
> written.** Test 4 replaces it with the total claim: `to_regclass('public.quiz_attempts')` is
> null, and no table in `public` has a `score`, `passed`, `points_awarded` or `attempt_no`
> column at all.

The one fact derivation cannot supply is elapsed time, which Tor's narrowing keeps. That is one
nullable column on the row that needs it, `answers.elapsed_ms`, and it is safe **only** because
a quiz requires named answers — so it can never become a timing channel on an anonymous
response. Test 5 asserts that pairing rather than trusting it.

## 3. THE LEADERBOARD IS A GATED AGGREGATE, NOT A SCOREBOARD

Taken without asking, because it follows from a confirmed decision. **The instinct that a quiz
on a named survey has nothing to protect is exactly the instinct the rule exists to override.**

Q28's line, in Tor's words: **«the line is the SUBJECT of the number, not its size.»** A count
of PEOPLE is participation and is not gated; anything derived from what those people SAID is.
**A team score is a per-group breakdown of which options people chose** — the most literal
available reading of «what they said» — and a team of two scoring full marks tells the room
that both of them got everything right. A leaderboard is a per-group breakdown wearing a game's
clothes.

So `public.quiz_leaderboard` gates on `app.k_for`, exactly as `aggregate_results` and
`get_quotes` do. `tests/invariants/threshold-policy.test.ts:441-462` would have failed it
otherwise, by construction, without naming it.

**A team below k is ABSENT — not zero, not a rank with «for få svar».** The next person will be
tempted to render a placeholder row for symmetry with the other gated surfaces; the test's
comment says why not, at length. It is the differencing insight: **a gated board that looks
complete leaks through its own silence.** Four teams listed where the company has six, two of
them marked «for få svar», tells the reader *which two* — and on a leaderboard that reads as
«which two did badly», because a small team that scored well is gated identically. The
threshold SENTENCE carries the honesty instead, rendered always (D42), because a sentence
saying «teams below k are not shown» discloses nothing and a row per hidden team discloses the
team.

## 4. Q83 — THE DEFAULT WAS TAKEN, AND ITS OWN MEASUREMENT REOPENED IT

Q83 is DEFAULT-SAFE and was taken as classified: four named tokens with a stated role. **What
the default also required was that the contrast be measured, and the measurement was the
finding** (D125).

| Token | Drawn hex | `--sf` on it |
|---|---|---|
| `--qz1` | `#F26B21` | **2.99 : 1** |
| `--qz2` | `#2F6FB0` | 5.13 : 1 |
| `--qz3` | `#2F7D4F` | 4.95 : 1 |
| `--qz4` | `#B0343C` | 6.06 : 1 |

I first read the tile as a coloured square with a decorative glyph, which would put it under
WCAG 1.4.11 (3:1) and make 2.99 a rounding argument. **Reading the markup rather than the
colour list says otherwise**: V2:3401-3405 puts the option LABEL on the tile at 15px bold —
11.25pt, *normal* text — so the requirement is **4.5:1**.

Nothing was restyled and the question went to Tor. **His decision: darken it.** `--qz1` is now
`#C14B0B` and is the only token in this project that is not the drawn hex. Hue and saturation
are the drawn colour's own, `hsl(21.2, 88.9%)`; only lightness moves, 53.9 % → 40.0 %, giving
**4.82:1**. Not the 41.4 % that first clears the line: that gives 4.53, three hundredths of
margin, which survives this calculation and might not survive another renderer's rounding.

## 6. FINDINGS

### 6.1 Standing question 1 bit, and the message-asserting rule caught it

The first statutory fixture took «any locked pack» and got `psykososial-kartlegging`, whose
policy forces `anonymity: anonymous`. The survey was therefore refused by rule **(1)**,
`quiz_requires_named` — **and the test would have passed on the wrong rule had it asserted only
«refused».** It asserts the message, so it failed. The fixture is now
`leverandor-apenhetsloven`, which is locked AND named, so only rule (2) can fire; test 2b
asserts that isolation rather than assuming it.

**That rule has now paid for itself twice.**

### 6.2 Every column added has a writer, asked in the migration

CLAUDE.md's standing question, applied on the way in after V2-9 produced it. `answer_index` and
`points` → `saveDraft` plus the answer-key control on quizzable cards; `quiz_time_bonus` and
`quiz_team_board` → `setQuizSettings`; `elapsed_ms` → `submit_response`. Tests 12–14 assert
each over the catalogue or the source.

### 6.3 `answer_index` has no default, deliberately

The bundle DISPLAYS an absent key as «Riktig svar: første alternativ» (V2:6507). A column
default of `0` would turn that display convenience into a **fabricated answer key on every
question ever written** — worse than an absent one, because it is indistinguishable in review
from a deliberate one. Test 7.

### 6.4 Two functions patched, both with the anchor asserted first

`M:0087` (`submit_response`) and `M:0088` (`get_survey_for_token`) each assert their anchor
occurs exactly once before replacing, and fail loudly with a hint naming D115 if the body has
moved. `M:0070` patched `send_round` at five occurrences of an uncounted anchor; tests 12c and
16 assert the outcome as well as the precondition.

**`submit_response`'s signature is unchanged**, deliberately: it is named in CLAUDE.md invariant
2, in the 5a3 allowlist, in `verify:attack` and in the catalogue sweep at
`tests/db/test-mode.test.ts` test 7. The timing rides in the existing `p_answers` payload.

### 6.5 The token payload carries `run_mode` and never the key

`get_survey_for_token` renders `/s/[token]`, so anything in that payload is in a network
response the respondent can read. `run_mode` is chrome. `answer_index` would hand every
respondent the correct answer, and test 15 asserts its absence from both the function body and a
real call.
