# 06 — What I would not conclude

**That the product is an HSE tool.** Seven statutory strings on four entry
screens is a small number in absolute terms. The finding is that the customer
count beside it is **zero**, and that a first impression is made of what is
present, not of a ratio. «Every screen tells them this is an HSE tool» is not
what was measured and I would not say it.

**That the drawing is at fault.** The v2 bundle is customer-first by 213 to 161
and puts the law in a trailing clause in its own HR entry. Whatever produced the
in-app imbalance, it was not the design intent.

**That the fixes in `03-findings.md` are ordered by value.** They are ordered by
confidence. Item 2 — Oversikt — is probably the most valuable and is the one I
could least measure, because it is a question about a rendered empty state.
**Ranking it below item 1 reflects what I know, not what matters.**

**That «0 av 0 plikter» actually renders.** I read the strings and the
conditionals; I did not see the screen. It may not render at all when the duty
set is empty. That is a capture question and it is UNVERIFIED.

**That the question bank's eleven categories are the whole bank.** Twelve
questions is small enough that it may be a fixture rather than the shipped
library, and `06-remainder.md` does not record an intended size. I measured what
is seeded and would not infer what was planned.

**That quiz looks good.** It is built, k-gated, tested and now reachable — and
nobody has seen it rendered, including me. The instruction hoped section 4 might
«surprise positively»; it surprised, but with a locked card rather than a
screenshot. **Whether the screens are worth demonstrating is still unknown**, and
the seed exists so that the first person with a browser can find out.

**That unlocking quiz is finished.** The card is unlocked, the copy is corrected
and the seed is written — none of it executed here. CI runs the seed in two jobs
and both database guards refuse a bad fixture loudly, so the run is the proof and
the run has not landed as this is written.

**That any of this is urgent relative to the mail and Turnstile work.** A buyer
meeting the wrong vocabulary is a slower loss than an unprotected anon-callable
write path. This review found a positioning problem in a product whose content is
right; that is a good problem, and it should not push out a security one.
